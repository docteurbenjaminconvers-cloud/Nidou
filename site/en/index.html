Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Do I have to scan everything myself?

No. You add papers as they come: a photo when a letter arrives, a forwarded email for an invoice. Lucien also recognises your contracts from bank debits and offers to add the missing paper.

I live alone: is Lucien for me?

Yes. Nothing makes you add anyone: the Solo plan covers one person. Papers, deadlines, budget, the Sunday round-up and the steps to follow when things go wrong all work the same. If one day you handle a relative's papers, you add them in one tap.

Can Lucien prepare my rental application?

Yes. For renting, family benefits, school, a loan, a passport or an insurance claim, Lucien gathers the papers asked for, flags those that are too old or missing, says where to get them, and prepares a single file with a cover sheet.

Does Lucien help with a move or a new baby?

Yes. In “Life situations”, choose what is happening and when: moving house, a birth, marriage or civil partnership, a separation, the death of a relative, a new car, losing a job, buying a home, getting a mortgage, a child leaving to study, retirement. Each step lands in your calendar on the right date, linked to your contracts, with the letters ready: change of address, notice to the landlord.

How long should I keep my papers?

Each paper says so, based on the periods published by the French administration: 5 years for an energy bill, 3 years for a tax notice, until retirement for payslips. Lucien tells you what can go, never a contract still running.

Can I see my deadlines in my phone's calendar?

Yes: subscribe once from the Agenda screen, in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Deadlines and the children's activities appear on their own, with a reminder beforehand.

What if the AI gets a date wrong?

Every extracted field is shown with its confidence level. You correct it with one tap and the reminders recalculate. No deadline is created on a date you couldn't see.

Does Lucien have access to my bank account?

You choose: import a statement (CSV or PDF) or connect your bank through a licensed aggregator (Enable Banking). In that case Lucien only receives the list of transactions, read-only. It can neither pay nor transfer, and never knows your credentials.

How does Lucien count savings?

Each action (cancel, renegotiate, drop a duplicate) carries an estimated yearly gain based on your own direct debits. The counter only adds a saving once it is proven: when the direct debit has disappeared from your statements after you cancelled. It never inflates with assumptions.

How is it different from a budgeting app?

A budgeting app tells you how much you spent. Lucien tells you what to cancel, before which date, how much you gain, and writes the letter. It links spending to contracts, which a budgeting app cannot do.

How does the AI connector work?

You add Lucien as a connector in your assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, and any MCP-compatible tool). The assistant can then consult your papers and deadlines to answer you. It cannot change anything, nor reach the folders you excluded. Every consultation appears in your log.

Can I manage my parents' paperwork?

Yes, with a paid plan: a space per person you help, rights per folder, and reminders that reach the carer. Two conditions, because the law protects the people being helped: the person must give consent in Lucien (and can withdraw it at any time), and their bank can only be connected by themselves or their legal representative. If they are under guardianship, the guardian authorises access, within the limits of their mandate.

Can I change my mind after paying?

Yes. For 14 days after a new subscription, “Withdraw” in Settings stops everything and refunds what you paid, minus the days used. After that, you cancel whenever you like: the subscription ends at the end of the paid period, at no cost.

What if I stop?

You export everything in one file, then delete your account. It is erased at the end of the period already paid, or straight away if you ask. No questions asked.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. Lucien is in French by default; you can switch it to English in Settings. Letters prepared for French organisations stay in French.
